## FFmpeg

Loavy Player downloads a pinned FFmpeg 4.4 Windows executable on demand and invokes it as a separate process for audio conversion. The selected build reports that it is GPL version 3 licensed. FFmpeg remains a separate work and is not covered by Loavy Player's license.
