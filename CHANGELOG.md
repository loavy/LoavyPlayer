# Changelog

## 12.6.0

- **Room (Jam)**: the page scrolls again with the mouse wheel and scrollbar.
- **Downloader**: on wide windows, a side column shows **Recent downloads** and short tips. The Download button keeps a normal size.
- Fixed a release where the Downloader and Spotify styles and the new glass look were left out of the build.

## 12.5.0

- **Glass**: panels follow the **Surface opacity** slider, the desktop shows through the gaps, and every panel has a lit edge and soft sheen. Light theme gets white frosted glass.
- **Downloader** redesign: one big link field that recognizes Spotify, YouTube, YouTube Music, SoundCloud and Bandcamp; compact rows for type, format and folder; a "Saved as" preview; a step-by-step progress card; "Try again" and "Repair tools" on errors; a **Recent downloads** list.
- **Spotify** redesign: Spotify green on its page and player bar, a large search bar, a **Playing on…** device picker, playlist cover cards, a playlist header built from the cover art, and moving bars on the song that's playing.

## 12.4.0

- **Room (Jam)** redesign: a live stage with a record sliding out of its sleeve and spinning while music plays, a red Live badge, listener avatars, and separate **Invite friends** and **In the room** cards. Setup uses two large choices, **Start a Jam** and **Join friends**.
- **Home** opens with a time-of-day greeting and a card to resume the last song.
- Small fixes in the full-screen player (queue count, status label) and a red Jam tag in the sidebar.

## 12.3.0

- New **Afterglow** design: plum-ink background, honey accent, floating panels, album-colored glow, Bahnschrift headings, a glowing play button and a dusk-to-honey progress line.
- **Accent presets** in Settings: Honey, Rose, Lilac, Lagoon, Fern and Ember, plus any custom color.
- `build-installer.cmd` builds the Windows installer and copies it to `Downloads\Loavy Player`.
